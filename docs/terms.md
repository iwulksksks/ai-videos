# Reelhouse: Terms of Service

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

Reelhouse is a personal tool. Its owner runs it on their own computer to make short cartoon videos for the channel "The Real Reason" and to post those videos to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

1. **Who can use it.** Reelhouse is used only by its owner, on their own accounts. It is not offered as a service to the public.
2. **What it does on TikTok.** With your permission, it uploads videos you made to your TikTok account, either to your TikTok inbox for you to finish posting, or as a post. It does nothing else on your account.
3. **Your control.** You choose what happens after each video, and you can disconnect TikTok at any time in Reelhouse or in TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions).
4. **TikTok's rules.** Everything posted must follow TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. AI-generated videos are labeled as AI-generated.
5. **No warranty.** Reelhouse is provided as-is, without any guarantee.
6. **Changes.** These terms may be updated on this page.
