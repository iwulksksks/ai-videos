# Reelhouse: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

Reelhouse is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used only by its owner, to post videos to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

**What it gets from TikTok.** When you connect TikTok, Reelhouse receives your TikTok display name and a sign-in token, so it can upload videos for you.

**Where it is kept.** Only on your own computer, in Reelhouse's local database. Nothing is sent to anyone else, sold, or shared.

**What it is used for.** Only to upload the videos you made, the way you chose in Reelhouse (to your TikTok inbox, or as a post), and to show which account is connected.

**Removing it.** Click **Disconnect** in Reelhouse to delete the sign-in token, or remove Reelhouse in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions).

**Changes.** This policy may be updated on this page.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/iwulksksks/ai-videos/issues>.
